Photography Tips

Capture the magic of Chefchaouen with these professional photography tips

Optimal Lighting

The blue walls of Chefchaouen look their best during the "golden hours" - early morning and late afternoon. The soft, warm light enhances the blue tones and creates beautiful shadows in the narrow streets.

Find Contrasts

Look for colorful elements that contrast with the blue walls - flower pots, carpets, doors, and local goods create striking color combinations and add interest to your photos.

Explore Side Streets

While the main streets are beautiful, the quieter side alleys often provide the most photogenic scenes. Wander off the main paths to discover hidden gems and unique compositions.

Camera Settings

The narrow streets can be challenging for exposure. Consider bracketing your shots or using HDR mode to capture both the bright skies and shadowed walls. Adjust your white balance to enhance the blues.

Experience Chefchaouen in Person

Photos can only capture a fraction of the beauty. Book a tour and experience the magic of the Blue Pearl with your own eyes.
