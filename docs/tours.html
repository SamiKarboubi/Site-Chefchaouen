Our Tour Experiences

Medina Walking Tour

3 hours

Explore the winding blue streets of Chefchaouen's historic medina with a knowledgeable local guide. Discover hidden corners, learn about the city's unique architecture, and visit key landmarks including the Kasbah, Grand Mosque, and Plaza Uta el-Hammam.

$35 per person Book Now

Cultural Immersion

5 hours

Experience the authentic culture of Chefchaouen with this immersive tour. Visit local artisans in their workshops, learn about traditional crafts, enjoy a cooking demonstration of Moroccan cuisine, and participate in a traditional tea ceremony.

$65 per person Book Now

Mountain Hiking

6 hours

Explore the stunning natural landscapes surrounding Chefchaouen with a guided hike in the Rif Mountains. Trek to the Spanish Mosque for panoramic views, continue to mountain streams and waterfalls, and enjoy a picnic lunch with local products.

$55 per person Book Now

Photography Tour

4 hours

Capture the most photogenic spots in the Blue Pearl with a guide who knows all the best angles and hidden viewpoints. Perfect for photography enthusiasts of all levels. Tour includes visits to scenic spots at the optimal time of day for lighting.

$45 per person Book Now

Culinary Tour

4 hours

Taste your way through Chefchaouen's delicious cuisine. Visit the local market to learn about ingredients, sample street food specialties, enjoy a traditional lunch, and finish with a cooking class where you'll learn to prepare authentic Moroccan dishes.

$70 per person Book Now

Akchour Day Trip

8 hours

Venture beyond Chefchaouen to the nearby natural paradise of Akchour. Hike through stunning landscapes to see the God's Bridge rock formation and the beautiful waterfalls. Swim in natural pools and enjoy lunch at a riverside restaurant.

$85 per person Book Now

Why Choose Our Tours

Local Guides

All our guides are local residents with deep knowledge of Chefchaouen's history, culture, and hidden gems.

Small Groups

We keep our tour groups small (maximum 8 people) to ensure a personalized experience and authentic interactions.

Authentic Experiences

Our tours go beyond the typical tourist routes to offer genuine cultural experiences and connections with local people.

Certified Guides

All our guides are officially certified by the Moroccan Ministry of Tourism and have extensive training in history, culture, and safety procedures. Many have been guiding in Chefchaouen for over a decade, providing unparalleled local expertise.

Multilingual Tours

We offer tours in multiple languages including English, French, Spanish, German, and Arabic. Our guides are fluent in at least three languages, ensuring clear communication and cultural understanding for international visitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our guides are fluent in English, French, Spanish, and Arabic. Some guides also speak German or Italian. If you need a guide for a specific language, please mention it in your booking request.

You can cancel up to 48 hours before your tour for a full refund. Cancellations less than 48 hours in advance are eligible for a 50% refund. No refunds are provided for cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance or no-shows. However, we understand that unexpected circumstances can arise, so please contact us if you need to cancel due to an emergency.

For all tours, we recommend comfortable walking shoes, a hat, sunscreen, and a water bottle. For mountain hiking tours, please also bring appropriate hiking footwear, layered clothing, and a small backpack. For photography tours, don't forget your camera equipment. Specific recommendations will be sent to you after booking.

Most of our tours are family-friendly and suitable for children. The Medina Walking Tour, Cultural Immersion, and Culinary Tour are great for families. The Mountain Hiking and Akchour Day Trip are recommended for children aged 8 and above who are comfortable with moderate hiking. Please let us know if you're traveling with children so we can tailor the experience accordingly.

After submitting your booking request, you'll receive a confirmation email with payment instructions. We accept credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for advance bookings. A 30% deposit is required to secure your reservation, with the balance payable on the day of the tour in cash (Moroccan Dirhams, Euros, or US Dollars) or by card.

Still have questions? Feel free to contact us!
