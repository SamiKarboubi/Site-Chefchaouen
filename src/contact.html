Frequently Asked Questions

You can book a tour through our website by visiting the Tours page and selecting your preferred experience. Alternatively, you can contact us directly via email, phone, or the contact form on this page. We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance, especially during peak tourist season.

Our standard cancellation policy allows for a full refund if you cancel at least 48 hours before your scheduled tour. Cancellations made less than 48 hours in advance are eligible for a 50% refund. No refunds are provided for cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance or no-shows. However, we understand that unexpected circumstances can arise, so please contact us if you need to cancel due to an emergency.

Our Location

From the Bus Station

A 15-minute walk through the new city. Follow Avenue Hassan II toward the medina entrance, then continue to Plaza Uta el-Hammam.

From Taxi Drop-off

Most taxis will drop you at Bab El-Ain gate. From there, it's a 5-minute walk uphill through the medina to reach our office at Plaza Uta el-Hammam.

Within the Medina

Our office is located in Plaza Uta el-Hammam, next to the Kasbah Museum. Look for the blue door with the "Chefchaouen Guide" sign.
